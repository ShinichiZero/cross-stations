✝ Stations of the Cross

A journey of prayer and reflection

Stations
of the Cross

Walk with Jesus through the fourteen moments of His Passion. Light a virtual candle at each station as a sign of your prayer.

Begin the Journey
I

Jesus is condemned to death

Pilate washes his hands yet the guilt remains with those who cried out. Jesus, though innocent, bows to unjust judgment. In every false accusation we bear, may we find the courage to trust in Your justice.

We adore You, O Christ, and we praise You, because by Your holy Cross You have redeemed the world.

II

Jesus takes up His Cross

The heavy wood is laid upon His shoulders — a weight that is not His own but ours. With each step He chooses love over escape. Teach us, Lord, to embrace the crosses in our lives with the same willing heart.

We adore You, O Christ, and we praise You, because by Your holy Cross You have redeemed the world.

III

Jesus falls the first time

The weight of the world brings Him to His knees, yet He rises. Weakness is not defeat. In our failures and falls, may we remember that He fell for us and rose again — and that His strength is made perfect in our frailty.

We adore You, O Christ, and we praise You, because by Your holy Cross You have redeemed the world.

IV

Jesus meets His Mother

Eyes meet across the crowd — the grief of a mother, the love of a Son. No words are needed. Mary walks this road beside Him still, interceding for us in every moment of sorrow. Hail, Holy Queen, Mother of Mercy.

We adore You, O Christ, and we praise You, because by Your holy Cross You have redeemed the world.

V

Simon of Cyrene carries the Cross

A stranger is pressed into service, yet that reluctant act of burden-sharing becomes eternal. Every time we help another in need — willingly or not — we walk in Simon's footsteps and help carry the Cross of Christ.

We adore You, O Christ, and we praise You, because by Your holy Cross You have redeemed the world.

VI

Veronica wipes the face of Jesus

One small act of compassion in the chaos of the crowd. Veronica steps forward with cloth in hand, and His image is imprinted on it forever. May we dare to act with kindness wherever we see suffering.

We adore You, O Christ, and we praise You, because by Your holy Cross You have redeemed the world.

VII

Jesus falls the second time

Again the earth meets His knees. The crowd presses on. He rises once more, not for Himself but for us. When we relapse into old wounds or old sins, His mercy rises with us. It never runs dry.

We adore You, O Christ, and we praise You, because by Your holy Cross You have redeemed the world.

VIII

Jesus meets the women of Jerusalem

"Daughters of Jerusalem, do not weep for me; weep for yourselves and for your children." Even in His darkest hour He turns outward, bearing witness to a truth greater than His suffering. May we learn to look beyond ourselves in our own pain.

We adore You, O Christ, and we praise You, because by Your holy Cross You have redeemed the world.

IX

Jesus falls the third time

Nearly at Golgotha, He falls once more. The finish line is near, yet the body is at its limit. He does not give up. For every moment we feel we cannot go on, His perseverance is the promise that grace is sufficient.

We adore You, O Christ, and we praise You, because by Your holy Cross You have redeemed the world.

X

Jesus is stripped of His garments

All dignity is taken from Him — yet He is most fully Himself: the Lamb offered freely. In humiliation and loss, when we feel stripped bare, may we find in His nakedness the cover of infinite worth.

We adore You, O Christ, and we praise You, because by Your holy Cross You have redeemed the world.

XI

Jesus is nailed to the Cross

The nails are driven through flesh — the very hands that healed the blind, raised the dead, blessed the children. Love, crucified. "Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do." May we echo that forgiveness in our own lives.

We adore You, O Christ, and we praise You, because by Your holy Cross You have redeemed the world.

XII

Jesus dies on the Cross

"It is finished." Three hours of darkness, and then the veil of the Temple is torn in two. Death is not the end — it is the door. In His last breath, every breath we will ever take was redeemed.

We adore You, O Christ, and we praise You, because by Your holy Cross You have redeemed the world.

XIII

Jesus is taken down from the Cross

Into His Mother's arms He is lowered. The Pietà — sorrow made visible. Mary holds the Saviour of the world, and her arms become the arms of the Church. We too are held in those moments when we are broken beyond words.

We adore You, O Christ, and we praise You, because by Your holy Cross You have redeemed the world.

XIV

Jesus is laid in the tomb

Silence. Stone. Darkness. But not the end. The seed that falls into the earth and dies bears much fruit. The tomb could not hold Him — and because He rose, neither death, nor sorrow, nor darkness has the final word in our lives.

We adore You, O Christ, and we praise You, because by Your holy Cross You have redeemed the world.

✝

May we carry our crosses with faith, hope, and love.

He is risen. Alleluia.
